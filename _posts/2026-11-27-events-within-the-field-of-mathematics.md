Just days ago, was the breakthrough of the Riemann hypothesis, and now the VP≠VNP news. I feel like the processes of these models themselves and their attainment of (rightfully mesmerizing) proofs is, itself, well-documented; however there is a deeper insight to discuss here, and that is: what does this mean for people within the field? Over the past few days, there have been answers to this broad question through many lenses, but I would like to answer it a bit optimistically. Human value will not be diminished from the field of mathematics (or any field for that matter), it will only to be transformed. Compute is finite, and going forwards, the identification of interesting and worthwhile problems and a sense of "Canonicalization" will remain human roles. These problems that have been solved are themselves problems defined by humans, and this initiation will remain as such. However, like all fields, the field of mathematics will continue to experience a dramatic transformation, and with that, all the people working in said field will also adapt to this new way of developing and advancing science.
